## Brand & Style

This design system translates the analog warmth of emotional journaling, tactile washi-tape scrapbooking, and colored-pencil/crayon illustration into a calming digital sanctuary. Crafted for young adults navigating relationships, attachment anxiety, and personal boundary-building, the aesthetic acts as an empathetic emotional mirror.

Drawing inspiration from tactile scrapbook craft and French-crayoned picture books, the visual language departs from cold, sterile productivity tooling. It replaces rigid geometry with gentle roundedness, soft cream paper grain, chalky colored-pencil strokes, and tape-stuck visual metaphors. The goal is to induce instant nervous-system decompression: an interface that feels like cracking open a beloved notebook under soft afternoon sunlight with a cup of warm tea.

Key emotional tenets:
- **Gentle Reassurance:** Soft tones and tactile textures validate emotional states without judgment or sterile gamification.
- **Cognitive Clarity:** Generous whitespace, thumb-reachable zones, and prominent focus states cut through emotional turbulence.
- **Physicality & Delight:** Torn paper contours, washi tape badges, and crayon-textured accents transform daily reflections into comforting physical artifacts.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the reference imagery: sunny citrus highlights, comforting pastel coral blushes, calm cobalt sky blues, and warm creamy fibrous paper. Dark mode is intentionally omitted to preserve the analog paper texture and authentic daylight illustration medium.

- **Primary (`#F4A247` - Citrus Zest):** The warm, optimistic anchor used for principal actions, active calendar highlights, daily check-in marks, and key energetic focal points.
- **Secondary (`#FF7563` - Coral Heart):** Tender pastel vermillion used for emotional self-love tags, priority alerts, heart milestones, and warm decorative accents. Supported by `#FFA07A` for lighter tint states.
- **Tertiary (`#245894` - Sky Lake Blue):** Stabilizing cobalt blue representing clarity, mental stillness, and objective thinking. Supported by `#4A7EB0` for soft washes and serene container backgrounds.
- **Neutral Surface / Canvas (`#FFFDF6` & `#FBF8F1`):** Sun-dappled parchment and unbleached cotton paper backgrounds that minimize eye strain and establish an organic substrate.
- **Neutral Outlines & Typography (`#2C4D75` & `#1E3A5F`):** Deep indigo sketch crayon replaces harsh pitch blacks. Used for pencil-drawn outlines, legible body copy, and navigation iconography.

## Typography

The pairing between `Bricolage Grotesque` and `Karla` synthesizes playful editorial personality with effortless legibility.

- **Headlines & Expressive Quotes:** `Bricolage Grotesque` brings bouncy warmth, off-kilter human curves, and friendly warmth that evokes boutique diary letterforms without sacrificing modern digital rhythm.
- **Body & Prompts:** `Karla` delivers an unpretentious, slightly eccentric grotesque rhythm with generous counters and relaxed letter widths, making lengthy stream-of-consciousness entries comfortable to write and reread.
- **Handwritten Accents:** For calendar dates, mood ratings, and sticker notes, small caps and slightly tracked labels mimic neat hand-annotated archival tabs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces mobile-first tactile interaction, prioritizing ample breathing room and one-handed thumb navigation. 

- **Grid & Safe Margins:** Uses a single-column fluid mobile canvas capped at a max-width of 540px for handheld viewports (scaling to multi-card masonry on tablet/desktop). Margins of 20px–24px leave clear, calm paper edges around journaling cards.
- **Breathing Space:** Elements are intentionally uncrowded. Sections are divided using generous vertical gaps (`space-xl`) or organic hand-drawn colored-pencil horizontal dividers rather than solid technical borders.
- **Thumb-Friendly Zones:** Primary action bars (such as "Today's Clarity Prompt" or "Log Mood") sit pinned or anchored within the bottom 35% thumb sweep area with minimum tap targets of 48px × 48px.

## Elevation & Depth

In place of sterile drop shadows or synthetic blurs, depth is expressed through the physical layering of stationery items:

1. **Base Parchment (`#FFFDF6`):** The foundational sketchbook spread, optionally infused with subtle noise or micro-fibrous grain.
2. **Layered Paper Notes (`#FBF8F1` or Tinted Pastel Washes):** Cards and entries cast subtle, offset colored-pencil shadows (`2px 4px 0px rgba(44, 77, 117, 0.12)` or `0 3px 8px rgba(44, 77, 117, 0.08)`). The shadow carries an indigo/cobalt hue rather than dirty gray.
3. **Pencil-Sketched Outlines:** Components feature delicate 1.5px to 2px solid outlines in `#2C4D75` or `#4A7EB0`, emulating 2B colored pencil sketches. When hovered or pressed, outlines slightly darken to `#1E3A5F`.
4. **Washi Tape & Stickers:** Modal heads, status chips, and milestone stickers sit rotated between `-1.5deg` and `+2deg`, casting tiny contact shadows (`1px 2px 2px rgba(44,77,117,0.15)`) to replicate translucent paper tape holding sheets together.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a Pill-shaped (`roundedness: 3`) geometry to convey friendliness, softness, and safety.

- **Base Radii:** Standard interactive chips, action pills, and status tags use full pill radiuses (`rounded-full` / 9999px). Cards and note modules utilize smooth large radii (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` / 24px–32px).
- **Washi Tape Cutouts:** Washi tabs on card headers feature subtle zigzag or semi-translucent torn end-caps with 4px border radii.
- **Organic Irregularity:** For featured elements (e.g., daily emotional status stickers or selected calendar avatars), subtle organic asymmetry (e.g., `border-radius: 28px 36px 30px 40px / 36px 28px 38px 32px`) is applied to preserve hand-made authenticity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Citrus Zest Pill):** Solid `#F4A247` fill with `#1E3A5F` bold text, a 1.5px `#2C4D75` sketch outline, and a soft offset shadow (`0 3px 0 #2C4D75`). On active/press, shifts down `2px` with shadow flattened.
- **Secondary Action (Tape / Outline Pill):** Semi-translucent `#FBF8F1` or `#FFA07A` with a 1.5px colored-pencil border `#2C4D75` and comforting `Karla` semi-bold copy.
- **Healing Action (Sky Calm Pill):** `#245894` background with crisp `#FFFDF6` cream text for definitive clarity confirmations.

### Cards & Daily Diary Prompts
- **Structure:** Rounded `24px` containers with unbleached cotton background (`#FBF8F1`), wrapped in a 1.5px soft `#2C4D75` border.
- **Tape Header:** Top-center or top-left decorative semi-transparent washi tape (`rgba(244, 162, 71, 0.45)` or `rgba(255, 117, 99, 0.4)`) overlapping the card edge by `12px`.
- **Inner Padding:** Generous 20px–24px internal padding keeping thoughts breathing comfortably.

### Chips & Emotion Stickers
- **Emotion Tags:** Pill-shaped tags (`height: 34px`) colored in soft tinted pastels (e.g., Peach Blush `#FFE6E0`, Sky Mist `#E3EEF8`, Daisy Lemon `#FEF5DC`).
- **Iconography:** Accompanied by micro hand-drawn motifs (little band-aids, cloud puffs, mini oranges, daisies, teacups).

### Calendar Matrix
- **Day Cells:** Rounded squares (`42px × 42px`) with soft hover states. Current day is encircled with a playful hand-drawn pencil oval or filled with `#F4A247` citrus circle.
- **Streak & Clarity Markers:** Marked by tiny crayon-textured heart stamps or lemon dots indicating days of emotional mindfulness.

### Checkboxes & Affirmation Radio Toggles
- **Affirmation Checkbox:** Rounded-md (`8px`) box with 2px `#2C4D75` crayon outline. When checked, fills with `#FF7563` and displays a bold, slightly imperfect white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Smooth concentric pill or circle that fills like an expressive wax crayon dab when selected.

### Input Fields & Reflection Textareas
- **Style:** Cream paper background with 1.5px `#2C4D75` hand-drawn outline, gently fading into a 10% `#245894` wash on focus.
- **Placeholder Text:** Light indigo `#4A7EB0` with a gentle, questioning tone (e.g., *"What is your heart telling you today?"*).
